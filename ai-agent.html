You remain in control

Useful autonomy with deliberate boundaries.

The agent is designed to accelerate routine work—not silently take control of sensitive workspace decisions.

◇No destructive actions

Deleting or removing workspace data remains a direct user action with the appropriate confirmation.

◇No role escalation

The agent cannot change member roles or modify role permissions. Sensitive access control stays in dedicated management screens.

◇Workspace-scoped context

Search and actions operate inside the selected workspace, with the app responsible for authentication and navigation.
